# Personas

> Based on 10 hosteller interviews, 2 staff interviews, and 24 survey responses (Weeks 3–5).

## Primary Persona: Aarav

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Aarav |
| **Age** | 20 |
| **Year** | 2nd year, Computer Science |
| **Hostel Block** | B6 |
| **Laundry frequency** | Weekly (every Monday) |
| **Tech comfort** | High — uses apps daily, comfortable with new tools |

### Bio

Aarav is a second-year CS student living in B6 block. He sends laundry every Monday morning and picks up Wednesday or Thursday — when he remembers. He's tried timing his pickups but has been burned too many times by clothes not being ready, so now he just checks every day until he finds them. He once lost a ₹600 jacket and stopped complaining after the warden said "there's no proof."

### Goals

1. Know exactly when his laundry will be ready — no guessing
2. Get a notification so he doesn't waste trips to the laundry room
3. Have a record of what he submitted, in case something goes wrong

### Frustrations

1. Zero visibility after drop-off — "it's a black hole"
2. Wasted trips — has gone 3 times in one week because he didn't know it wasn't ready
3. No recourse when items are lost — "no receipt, no proof, no justice"
4. Different blocks have different schedules — confusing when friends talk about their laundry days

### Needs

1. Real-time status tracking (like Swiggy for laundry)
2. Notification when clothes are ready for pickup
3. Submission record with item details

### Behaviors

- How they currently track: Doesn't. Just goes to check every day around pickup time.
- What they do when something goes wrong: Complained once, gave up after no resolution.
- Tech-savviness: High. Would adopt an app immediately if it existed.

### Quotes from Interviews

> "It's a black hole. You put clothes in, you get clothes out — maybe." — H01

> "Just tell me when it's done. That's literally all I want." — H01 (survey)

## Secondary Persona: Priya

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Priya |
| **Age** | 21 |
| **Year** | 3rd year, Electronics |
| **Hostel Block** | C3 |
| **Laundry frequency** | Bi-weekly |
| **Tech comfort** | Medium — uses phone for social media, not heavy app user |

### Bio

Priya is a third-year student who's been burned by the laundry system too many times. She once had a white top come back with a blue stain (washed with dark clothes) and when she complained, staff said "we wash everything the same way." She now sorts her clothes meticulously before giving them, and has given up on complaining about issues. She wants things to work but has low expectations.

### Goals

1. Submit laundry with confidence that it won't be damaged or mixed up
2. Know the turnaround time before she drops off
3. Have a simple way to report issues when they happen

### Frustrations

1. Clothes mixed up or stained — no accountability
2. No confirmation of what she submitted — "how do I prove I gave 5 shirts?"
3. Staff dismissal when she reports issues — "they just say 'we don't know'"
4. No way to hold staff accountable without evidence

### Needs

1. Item-level tracking (what was submitted, when, current status)
2. Structured complaint system (not just "talk to staff")
3. Photo evidence at drop-off (to prove what was submitted)

### Behaviors

- How they currently track: Sorts clothes herself, takes photos of items before giving them (for her own records, not for any system)
- What they do when something goes wrong: Takes photos, but has nowhere to submit them. Eventually gives up.
- Tech-savviness: Medium. Would use a simple app but won't adopt anything complex.

### Quotes from Interviews

> "I took a photo of the stain but... what do I do with it? There's no one to show." — H04

> "The worst part is not knowing. You drop your clothes and just... wait. And hope." — H02

## Staff Persona: Rajesh

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Rajesh (S01) |
| **Role** | Head Laundry Staff, B6 |
| **Experience** | 8 years |
| **Daily load** | ~40 students (peak), ~20 (off-peak) |

### Bio

Rajesh runs the B6 laundry with one assistant. He's experienced and efficient but frustrated by the constant "is it done?" interruptions from students. He wants to help but has no tools to communicate status. He uses a notebook to log names — it works but isn't foolproof. He'd welcome a simple system that lets him update status and students see it.

### Goals

1. Reduce "is it done?" interruptions from students
2. Have a simple way to log what each student submitted
3. Be able to notify students when batch is ready

### Frustrations

1. Students come to ask "is my laundry done?" 10+ times per drop-off day
2. No way to batch-notify students — "I don't have their numbers"
3. Disputes are always "he-said-she-said" — no records to reference
4. Machine breakdowns cause delays but students don't know why

### Needs

1. One-tap status update ("batch is ready")
2. Automatic notification to students when status changes
3. Simple item log per student

### Quotes from Interviews

> "If students could see 'washing' or 'ready,' they'd stop coming to ask." — S01

> "If there was a button I could press to say 'batch is ready' and it automatically tells students, that would save me so much time." — S02
