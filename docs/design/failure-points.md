## Top 5 Failure Points to Address

Based on research validation (frequency × severity × user desire):

1. **FP-14: No channel for status updates** — 100% frequency, 5/5 severity. Students have zero visibility. 79% want a tracking app. **→ Real-time status tracking**
2. **FP-06: Ready notification** — 100% frequency, 5/5 severity. Students waste trips. 58% want "ready" notifications. **→ Push notifications at stage transitions**
3. **FP-11/FP-12: No dispute system + no evidence** — 100% frequency, 5/5 severity. 33% give up on disputes. **→ Structured complaint form + digital audit trail**
4. **FP-01: No confirmation/receipt** — 100% frequency, 4/5 severity. No record of submission. **→ Digital submission receipt + item log**
5. **FP-13: Peak load delays** — 67% frequency, 3/5 severity. Unpredictable turnaround. **→ Custom timings + ETA estimates**
