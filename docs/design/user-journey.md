# User Journey Map

> Mapped from the student's perspective using interview quotes, survey data, and observation notes (Weeks 3–5).

---

## Journey Stages

```
Set Up Timings → Decide to Do Laundry → Drop Off → Wait → Check Status → Pick Up → Post-Pickup → (Dispute if needed)
```

### Detailed Journey

| Stage | What the student does | What happens backstage | Emotion | Pain Points | Opportunities |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Setting up laundry timings** | Figures out their block's laundry days (asks friends, checks notice board). No centralized info. | — | 😐 Confused | Every hostel is different. No app accounts for this. Schedule changes aren't communicated. | **Custom timings** — let student configure their own schedule |
| **1. Decide to do laundry** | Gathers dirty clothes. Checks if it's a drop-off day for their block. | — | 😐 Neutral | Sometimes doesn't know if today is a drop-off day. | **Dashboard shows "Next drop-off: Mon 9 AM"** |
| **2. Dropping off** | Takes bag to laundry room during drop-off window. Hands to staff. Gets no receipt. | Staff tag with tape + initials. Log name in notebook. Sort by color. | 😟 Anxious | No confirmation. No receipt. Doesn't know turnaround. Staff say "2-3 days" vaguely. | **Submission confirmation with ETA** |
| **3. Hand-off / Tagging** | Gives clothes, gets nothing visible in return. Walks away hoping for the best. | Staff tag bags with tape, write initials. Sort by color. Load machines. | 😟 Anxious | No proof of submission. Tags are fragile (tape + marker). | **QR code / digital receipt at drop-off** |
| **4. Waiting** | Has no idea where clothes are. Goes about day hoping. | Washing → Drying → Folding. Machines may break. Rain may delay drying. | 😟→😟 Anxious + helpless | Zero visibility. No ETA. No way to check status without physically going. | **Real-time status tracking** |
| **5. Checking status** | Walks to laundry room to check if done. If not, walks back. May repeat 2-3 times. | Staff may or may not know exact status. | 😟→😟 Frustrated | Wasted trips. Multiple check-ins. Staff overwhelmed by "is it done?" questions. | **App shows current status — no trip needed** |
| **6. Being told it's ready** | Hears from someone, or sees bag on shelf. No notification. | Staff place bags on shelf. No communication system. | 😐 Uncertain | Doesn't know until they physically check. May miss the window. | **Push notification: "Ready for pickup!"** |
| **7. Picking up** | Goes to shelf, finds bag by checking tags. No ID verification. | Staff may help locate. | 😊 Relieved (if found) | Bag may be hard to find. Wrong bags mixed up. No verification. | **Named/numbered bags, claim system** |
| **8. Checking items** | Opens bag, verifies contents. Sometimes finds missing/damaged items. | — | 😊 or 😟 Depends | May discover issues too late. No way to prove what was submitted. | **Photo proof at drop-off for comparison** |
| **9. Finding something wrong** | Notices missing/damaged item. Doesn't know who to talk to. | Staff may or may not help. No records. | 😡 Frustrated | No structured complaint process. Memory-vs-memory. No evidence. | **Structured dispute form with evidence** |
| **10. Trying to resolve** | Goes to staff → "we don't know." Goes to warden → "talk to staff." Gives up. | Staff have no records. Warden has no visibility. | 😡→😞 Resigned | Dead end. No resolution path. No accountability. | **Digital audit trail + escalation tiers** |

---

## Key Moments of Truth

1. **Drop-off:** Do I get confirmation? Do I know the timeline? → Currently: NO. Opportunity: Digital receipt + ETA.
2. **Waiting:** Can I check status? Do I know when to expect pickup? → Currently: NO. Opportunity: Real-time tracking.
3. **Dispute:** Is there a clear, fair way to report and resolve issues? → Currently: NO. Opportunity: Structured complaint system.

---

## Current vs. Future State

| Stage | Current Experience | Future Experience (with app) |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Ask friends, check notice board | Configure once in app — schedule adapts to your hostel |
| Drop-off | Hand over bag, get nothing | Submit in app → get confirmation + ETA |
| Waiting | Zero visibility, just wait | See status: Submitted → Received → Washing → Drying → Ready |
| Checking | Walk to laundry room 2-3 times | Check app — no wasted trips |
| Pickup | Rumage through bags on shelf | Get notification → go directly → claim bag |
| Dispute | "Talk to staff" → "talk to warden" → give up | Structured form → digital record → escalation path |
