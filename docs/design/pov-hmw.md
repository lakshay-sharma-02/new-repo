# POV Statements & HMW

> Framed from user perspective using research evidence (Weeks 3–5).

---

## POV Formula

> **[User]** needs **[need]** because **[insight]**.

---

## POV Statements (Research-Validated)

| # | POV Statement | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| POV-1 | "A hosteller needs real-time laundry tracking because they have zero visibility after drop-off and waste 2-3 trips per cycle checking if clothes are ready." | 10/10 interviews, Survey Q8 (75% arrived to find clothes not ready), Observations (all 3 cycles) |
| POV-2 | "A hosteller needs pickup notifications because 54% have missed pickup windows due to not knowing clothes were ready." | Survey Q9 (54% said yes), H07: "I went 3 times in one week" |
| POV-3 | "A hosteller needs predictable turnaround times because laundry delays are unpredictable (1.9/5 satisfaction) and cause anxiety when clean clothes are needed." | Survey Q6 (1.9/5), 8/10 interviews cited unpredictability |
| POV-4 | "A hosteller needs a submission record because there's no receipt or proof when disputes arise, leaving students helpless against memory-vs-memory claims." | 6/10 interviews, H04: "lost jacket, no proof, no justice", W01: "I wish I had a record" |
| POV-5 | "A hosteller needs a configurable schedule because every hostel block has different laundry days, times, and turnaround — one-size-fits-all doesn't work." | 5/10 interviews, H03: "B6 is Mon/Wed/Fri, C3 is Tue/Thu/Sat" |

---

## HMW Questions (derived from POVs)

| # | HMW Question | Linked POV |
|---|---|---|
| HMW-1 | How might we give students real-time visibility into their laundry status so they stop wasting trips? | POV-1 |
| HMW-2 | How might we notify students the moment clothes are ready so they don't miss pickup windows? | POV-2 |
| HMW-3 | How might we set expectations for turnaround time so students aren't frustrated by unpredictable delays? | POV-3 |
| HMW-4 | How might we create a submission record so disputes have evidence instead of memory-vs-memory? | POV-4 |
| HMW-5 | How might we let students configure their own hostel's laundry schedule so the app works for any block? | POV-5 |

---

## Final HMW (Project Focus)

> **How might we make hostel laundry predictable and accountable for both the student and the service?**

This is refined from the original project brief. It encompasses:
- **Predictable** → tracking + ETA + notifications (HMW-1, HMW-2, HMW-3)
- **Accountable** → submission records + dispute system (HMW-4)
- **For both** → student sees status, staff can update it, warden has records

---

## Key Insight (from research)

> **The core problem isn't that laundry is bad — it's that the process is invisible.** Students can't see where their clothes are, staff can't communicate status, and disputes have no evidence. Making the process visible (through tracking) solves the majority of frustrations.
