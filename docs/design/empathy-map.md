### THINKS
- "Will my clothes be ready today, or will I waste another trip?"
- "I hope nothing is missing or damaged this time"
- "Should I go check now or wait another hour?"
- "There's no point complaining — nothing will change"
- "I wish I knew where my clothes were in the process"
- "Different blocks have different schedules — which one is mine?"

### FEELS
- **Anxiety** during the wait — no ETA, no visibility
- **Frustration** when making wasted trips (clothes not ready)
- **Helplessness** when something goes wrong (lost/damaged items)
- **Resignation** — "this is just how it works here"
- **Distrust** of the laundry service after bad experiences
- **Embarrassment** when asking staff repeatedly

### SAYS
- "Is my laundry done yet?" (asks staff 2–3 times per drop-off day)
- "I'll just go check again" (after being told "not yet")
- "What's the point of complaining? They'll just say 'we don't know'"
- "Just tell me when it's ready — that's all I want"
- "Last time they lost my shirt and nothing happened"

### DOES
- Goes to laundry room **2–3 times** to check if clothes are ready
- Takes photos of clothes before submitting (for personal records)
- Asks friends "is yours done?" as a proxy for their own status
- Gives up on disputes after first failed attempt
- Sorts clothes before giving (some students — speeds up processing)
- Checks WhatsApp groups for any laundry updates (there are none)

## Pain Points (from research)

| # | Pain Point | Frequency | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No visibility into laundry status after drop-off | 10/10 hostellers | 5/5 |
| 2 | Unpredictable turnaround time | 8/10 hostellers | 4/5 |
| 3 | Wasted trips to check if clothes are ready | 7/10 hostellers | 4/5 |
| 4 | No recourse when items are lost/damaged | 6/10 hostellers | 5/5 |
| 5 | Clothes mixed up or stained | 6/10 hostellers | 3/5 |
| 6 | No confirmation/receipt at drop-off | 5/10 hostellers | 3/5 |
| 7 | Different hostels have different schedules (confusing) | 5/10 hostellers | 2/5 |
| 8 | Peak days cause long delays | 4/10 hostellers | 3/5 |

## Gain Points (what would delight them)

| # | Gain | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real-time status tracking (like food delivery) | Survey: 79% want it, 7/10 interviewees described this |
| 2 | Notification when clothes are ready | Survey: "ready" notification was top choice (58%) |
| 3 | ETA at drop-off | H01, H06, H13 explicitly requested this |
| 4 | Simple complaint form (not "talk to staff") | H04, H09, W01 described need for structured disputes |
| 5 | Item-level tracking (prove what was submitted) | H04 (took photos), S01 (notebook logging) |
