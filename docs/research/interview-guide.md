### Key Themes from Interviews

#### Theme 1: Zero Visibility After Drop-off (9/10 hostellers)

Every hosteller independently described the same frustration: after handing over clothes, they have **absolutely no way** to know where their clothes are in the process.

> "It's a black hole. You put clothes in, you get clothes out — maybe." — H04

> "I literally check the laundry room 2–3 times because I don't know when it's done." — H07

> "There's no system. You just... hope." — H02

#### Theme 2: Unpredictable Turnaround (8/10 hostellers)

Turnaround varies from same-day to 5+ days. Students can't plan around it.

> "Sometimes it's ready the same day, sometimes it takes 3 days. There's no pattern." — H01

> "I stopped making plans around laundry because I can never predict when it'll be done." — H08

> "The worst is when you NEED clean clothes and you have no idea if they'll be ready." — H03

#### Theme 3: Wasted Trips (7/10 hostellers)

Students make multiple trips to check if clothes are ready because there's no notification.

> "I went on Tuesday, Wednesday, AND Thursday. It was finally ready Thursday evening." — H07

> "I just go every day at pickup time now. It's the only way to know." — H05

#### Theme 4: Disputes Are Hopeless (6/10 hostellers)

When something goes wrong (lost, damaged, mixed up), there's no structured way to report it.

> "My shirt had a stain. I told staff. They said 'we wash everything the same.' What am I supposed to do?" — H11 (from observations)

> "I lost a jacket worth ₹800. No receipt, no proof, no one to complain to. Just gone." — H04

> "The warden said 'talk to staff.' Staff said 'talk to the warden.' I gave up." — H09

#### Theme 5: They Want a Simple Tracking App (7/10 hostellers)

Most hostellers described wanting something exactly like what we built — a simple stage-based tracker.

> "Just give me a screen that says 'washing' or 'ready.' Like Swiggy for laundry." — H06

> "Even a WhatsApp message when it's done would be life-changing." — H15 (survey)

> "I don't need anything fancy. Just tell me when I can pick it up." — H01

#### Theme 6: Different Hostels, Different Schedules (5/10 hostellers)

Students from different blocks have completely different laundry days, times, and turnaround expectations.

> "In B6 it's Mon/Wed/Fri, but my friend in C3 says theirs is Tue/Thu/Sat." — H03

> "The timings changed last semester and nobody told us. I showed up on the wrong day." — H08

## Staff Interview Summary (S01–S02)

### S01: Head Laundry Staff, B6 (8 years experience)

**Key insights:**
- Processes ~40 students' laundry per day during peak
- Uses a notebook to log names — "it works for us, but it's not perfect"
- Would welcome a simple status update system: "If students could see 'washing' or 'ready,' they'd stop coming to ask"
- Biggest challenge: "Peak days. Friday and Monday, everyone comes at once."
- On disputes: "I can't prove what was in a bag. Neither can they. It's always a he-said-she-said."

> "I'm not against technology. If it makes my job easier, I'll use it." — S01

### S02: Laundry Staff, C3 (2 years experience)

**Key insights:**
- Handles ~25 students' laundry per day
- Machine breakdowns happen ~1/month, causing 30–60 min delays
- Weather is a real problem — "If it rains during drying, everything gets delayed"
- Would love a way to notify students: "I wish I could just send a message saying 'done' but I don't have their numbers"
- On lost items: "Sometimes things fall between bags. We try our best, but there's no system."

> "If there was a button I could press to say 'batch is ready' and it automatically tells students, that would save me so much time." — S02

## Warden Interview (W01)

**Warden, B6 + A2 blocks**

- Receives ~3–4 laundry complaints per month
- Most common: "clothes missing" or "clothes damaged"
- Resolution is always manual — talks to staff, asks student for details
- "I wish I had a record. Right now it's my word against the student's."
- Would support a digital logging system: "Even a simple spreadsheet would help."

> "A tracking system would reduce complaints to me by at least half." — W01
