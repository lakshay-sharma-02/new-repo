# Observations Log

> **Goal:** Follow 3 complete laundry cycles end-to-end, documenting each step, timing, and failure point from the student's and staff's perspective.

---

## Observation Protocol

For each cycle, record:
1. **Date and time** of each stage
2. **What happens** at each step (who does what, what systems are used)
3. **Visible pain points** (confusion, delays, errors)
4. **Student experience** (frustration, uncertainty, trust signals)
5. **Staff experience** (workarounds, pain points, shortcuts)

---

## Cycle 1: Wednesday, Week 3

### Setup
- **Block:** B6
- **Day of week:** Wednesday
- **Time slot:** Morning (drop-off window)
- **Weather/season:** Clear, mild (October)
- **Laundry schedule for this block:** Mon / Wed / Fri, 9 AM – 12 PM drop-off, 4 PM – 6 PM pickup

### Timeline — Track Exact Times

| Time | Stage | What Happens | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9:15 AM | **Drop-off** | 8 students arrive within 15 min. Each hands over a bag. Staff (2 people) tag bags with colored tape + marker pen writing initials. No receipt given. | One student asks "how long will it take?" — staff says "2 days, maybe 3" |
| 9:45 AM | **Sorting** | Staff opens bags, sorts by color (whites vs. darks). No individual tracking beyond the tape tag. | 2 bags had similar initials (AK) — staff had to ask which was which |
| 10:30 AM | **Washing** | 3 machine loads run. Each machine holds ~4 students' clothes mixed together. | Staff says they "try to keep block-wise" but don't always |
| 12:15 PM | **Drying** | Clothes hung on rooftop lines. No covers — open air. | Weather was clear, but staff admits rain has ruined batches before |
| 3:00 PM (Thu) | **Folding** | 1 staff member folds. Sorts back into tagged bags. | Staff folds 3 bags wrong — puts Aarav's shirt into Priya's bag. Catches it at the end |
| 4:30 PM (Thu) | **Ready for pickup** | Bags placed on shelf. No notification sent. Students don't know unless they physically come check. | 3 students came at 3 PM — clothes weren't ready. They'll have to come back |
| 5:15 PM (Thu) | **Pickup** | Students come, find bags by checking tags. No ID verification. | One student takes wrong bag — realizes at hostel, brings it back |

### Timing Observations (Key!)

| Question | Observation |
|---|---|
| What is the official laundry schedule (days/hours)? | Mon / Wed / Fri, 9 AM – 12 PM drop-off, 4 PM – 6 PM pickup |
| Did the actual turnaround match what students expected? | Students expected same-day. Turnaround was ~31 hours (not same-day). |
| Did students know clothes were ready BEFORE coming? | No. 3 students came early and had to return. |
| Were there any students who came to check and clothes weren't ready? | Yes — 3 out of 8 students. |
| How long did students wait before coming to check? | Most came around 4:30 PM (30 min before window closed) hoping it'd be ready. |

### Key Observations

- **Zero visibility.** After dropping off, students have NO way to know where their clothes are. They just guess.
- **No receipts.** If a dispute arises, there's no record of what was submitted or when.
- **Wrong bag incidents** happen regularly — the tagging system (tape + initials) is fragile.
- **Peak confusion** at pickup — students rummage through bags looking for theirs.
- **Staff are overwhelmed** during peak hours (9–11 AM on drop-off days).

- **Day of week:** Monday
- **Time slot:** Morning
- **Weather/season:** Clear, warm (early November)

| Question | Observation |

## Cross-Cycle Patterns

| Pattern | Cycles observed in | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Students have ZERO visibility after drop-off | All 3 cycles | High — causes repeated wasted trips, anxiety |
| No receipt or submission record exists | All 3 cycles | High — disputes have no evidence |
| Wrong items / missing items have no resolution | Cycles 1, 2, 3 | High — students give up or escalate to warden |
| Staff are friendly but have no tools to communicate status | All 3 cycles | Medium — they want to help but can't |
| Machine breakdowns / weather cause unpredictable delays | Cycles 1, 2 | Medium — students can't adjust plans |
| Peak drop-off days (Fri, Mon) overwhelm staff | Cycles 2, 3 | Medium — more errors, slower processing |
| Self-sorting by students significantly speeds up processing | Cycle 3 | Low — easy win, no incentive to do it |
| No notification system — "just come check" is the only option | All 3 cycles | High — core problem |
