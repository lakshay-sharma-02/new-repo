## Section 4: Open-ended Responses

### Q17: Biggest frustration with hostel laundry

**Theme frequency analysis (24 responses):**

| Theme | Count | % |
|---|---|---|
| No visibility / can't track status | 16 | 67% |
| Unpredictable turnaround | 12 | 50% |
| Clothes lost / damaged with no resolution | 9 | 38% |
| No notification when ready | 8 | 33% |
| Wrong items / mixed up | 7 | 29% |
| Having to physically check multiple times | 6 | 25% |

**Selected quotes:**

> "The worst part is not knowing. You drop your clothes and just... wait. And hope." — H02

> "I've gone to pick up 3 times in one week and it wasn't ready any of those times." — H07

> "They lost my jacket and when I complained, they said 'we don't know.' What kind of system is that?" — H11

> "Even a simple WhatsApp message saying 'done' would change everything." — H15

### Q18: Describe a time you had no idea where your laundry was

> "I dropped clothes on Monday. It was Thursday and still not ready. I asked staff and they said 'maybe tomorrow.' I had no clean shirts for class." — H04

> "I submitted on Friday, forgot about it, and picked it up the following Wednesday. Nobody said anything. It just sat there for 4 days." — H09

### Q19: Design the perfect laundry experience

> "Just tell me when it's done. That's literally all I want." — H01

> "An app like Swiggy but for laundry — show me where my clothes are." — H06

> "Give me a time estimate at drop-off. Even if it's wrong, at least I have something to go on." — H13

## Key Quantitative Findings

1. **Satisfaction is low:** 2.3/5 average — 58% rated 1 or 2
2. **Turnaround is unpredictable:** 1.9/5 — 75% rated 1 or 2
3. **79% want a tracking app** — strong demand
4. **Top need: Real-time tracking** (75% ranked it #1)
5. **67% cite "no visibility"** as their biggest frustration
6. **54% have missed pickup** because they didn't know clothes were ready
7. **67% have experienced** at least one laundry issue (lost/damaged/mixed/wrong)

*Survey conducted Weeks 4–5. 24 responses collected across 5 hostel blocks.*
